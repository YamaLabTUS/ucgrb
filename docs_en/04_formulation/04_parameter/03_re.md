# Parameters for renewable energy

## Parameters related to PV
$$
\begin{array}{ll}
      C_{a,\text{PV}}^{\text{suppr}}
       & : \text{Cost of PV suppression in area $a$ [kYen/MWh]}
      \\
      P_{t,a,\text{PV}}^{\text{upper}}
       & : \text{Upper limit of a forecasted interval of PV output in area $a$ at time $t$ [MW] (becomes actual output value when planning on the same day)}
      \\
      P_{t,a,\text{PV}}^{\text{output}}
       & : \text{PV output in area $a$ at time $t$ [MW] (becomes output forecast value at the time of the previous day’s planning)}
      \\
      P_{t,a,\text{PV}}^{\text{lower}}
       & : \text{Lower limit of a forecasted interval of PV output in area $a$ at time $t$ [MW] (becomes actual output value when planning on the same day)}
      \\
      R_{t,a,\text{PV}}^{\text{GF\\&LFC}\,\text{UP}}
       & : \text{Forecasted error rate of the up-ward trend in short-period (GF\\&LFC component) variation of PV output at time $t$ in area $a$ [\\%]}
      \\
      R_{t,a,\text{PV}}^{\text{GF\\&LFC}\,\text{DOWN}}
       & : \text{Forecasted error rate of the down-ward trend in short-period (GF\\&LFC component) variation of PV output at time $t$ in area $a$ [\\%]}
      \\
      R_{a,\text{PV}}^{\text{res}\,\text{UP}}
       & : \text{Rate of PV capacity of providing up-reserve in area $a$ [\\%]}
      \\
      R_{a,\text{PV}}^{\text{res}\,\text{DOWN}}
       & : \text{Rate of PV capacity of providing down-reserve in area $a$ [\\%]}
\end{array}
$$
