# Parameters for area

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
      C_{a}^{\text{short}}
       & : \text{Power supply shortage cost per unit in area $a$ [kYen/MWh]}
      \\
      C_{a}^{\text{surplus}}
       & : \text{Power supply surplus cost per unit in area $a$ [kYen/MWh]}
      \\
      C_{a}^{\text{tert,short}}
       & : \text{Tertiary reserve shortage cost in area $a$ [kYen/MWh]}
      \\
      D_{t,a}
       & : \text{Power demand in area $a$ at time $t$ [MW]}
      \\
      P_{t,a,\text{Others}}
       & : \text{Output of other power generation facilities in area $a$ at time $t$ [MW]}
      \\
      R_{t,a}^{\text{GF\\&LFC}\,\text{UP}}
       & : \text{Short-period (GF\\&LFC component) variation forecast up error rate of electricity demand for area $a$ at time $t$ [\\%]}
      \\
      R_{t,a}^{\text{GF\\&LFC}\,\text{DOWN}}
       & : \text{Short-period (GF\\&LFC component) variation forecast down error rate of electricity demand for area $a$ at time $t$ [\\%]}
      \\
      M_{t,a}^{\text{req}}
       & : \text{Minimum secured per-unit system inertia constant for area $a$ at time $t$ [MW $\cdot$ s/MVA]}
\end{array}
$$
