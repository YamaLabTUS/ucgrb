# Parameters for tie line

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
      C_{tie}^{\text{penalty}}
       & : \text{Penalty coefficient for interchange power in tie line $tie$ [kYen/MWh]}
      \\
      C_{tie}^{\text{GF\\&LFC}\,\text{UP, penalty}}
       & : \text{Penalty coefficient for interchange GF\\&LFC up-reserve in tie line $tie$ [kYen/MWh]}
      \\
      C_{tie}^{\text{GF\\&LFC}\,\text{DOWN, penalty}}
       & : \text{Penalty coefficient for interchange GF\\&LFC down-reserve in tie line $tie$ [kYen/MWh]}
      \\
      C_{tie}^{\text{Tert}\,\text{UP, penalty}}
       & : \text{Penalty coefficient for interchange tertiary up-reserve in tie line $tie$ [kYen/MWh]}
      \\
      C_{tie}^{\text{Tert}\,\text{DOWN, penalty}}
       & : \text{Penalty coefficient for interchange tertiary down-reserve in tie line $tie$ [kYen/MWh]}
      \\
      P_{t,tie}^{\text{TTC, forward}}
       & : \text{Forward total transfer capability of tie line $tie$ at time $t$ [MW]}
      \\
      P_{t,tie}^{\text{TTC, counter}}
       & : \text{Counter total transfer capability of tie line $tie$ at time $t$ [MW]}
      \\
      P_{t,tie}^{\text{Margin, forward}}
       & : \text{Forward margin of tie line $tie$ at time $t$ [MW]}
      \\
      P_{t,tie}^{\text{Margin, counter}}
       & : \text{Counter margin of tie line $tie$ at time $t$ [MW]}
      \\
      P_{t,tie}^{\text{GF\\&LFC}\,\text{UP, forwardMAX}}
       & : \text{Maximum GF\\&LFC up-reserve of tie line $tie$ at time $t$ [MW]}
      \\
      P_{t,tie}^{\text{GF\\&LFC}\,\text{UP, counterMAX}}
       & : \text{Maximum value of counter GF\\&LFC up-reserve of tie line $tie$ at time $t$ [MW]}
      \\
      P_{t,tie}^{\text{GF\\&LFC}\,\text{DOWN, forwardMAX}}
       & : \text{Maximum value of forward GF\\&LFC down-reserve of tie line $tie$ at time $t$ [MW]}
      \\
      P_{t,tie}^{\text{GF\\&LFC}\,\text{DOWN, counterMAX}}
       & : \text{Maximum value of counter GF\\&LFC down-reserve of tie line $tie$ at time $t$ [MW]}
      \\
      P_{t,tie}^{\text{Tert}\,\text{UP, forwardMAX}}
       & : \text{Maximum value of forward tertiary up-reserve of tie line $tie$ at time $t$ [MW]}
      \\
      P_{t,tie}^{\text{Tert}\,\text{UP, counterMAX}}
       & : \text{Maximum value of counter tertiary up-reserve of tie line $tie$ at time $t$ [MW]}
      \\
      P_{t,tie}^{\text{Tert}\,\text{DOWN, forwardMAX}}
       & : \text{Maximum value of forward tertiary down-reserve of tie line $tie$ at time $t$ [MW]}
      \\
      P_{t,tie}^{\text{Tert}\,\text{DOWN, counterMAX}}
       & : \text{Maximum value of counter tertiary down-reserve of tie line $tie$ at time $t$ [MW]}
\end{array}
$$
