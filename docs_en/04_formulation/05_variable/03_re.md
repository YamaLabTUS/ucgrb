# Decision variables for renewable energy

Unless otherwise stated, all variables x are non-negative real numbers. $x \in R_{+}$ (non-negative real numbers)

## Decision variables related to PV
$$
\begin{array}{ll}
      p_{t,a,\text{PV}}^{\text{suppr}}
       & : \text{Average amount of PV output suppression in area $a$ at time $t$ [MW]}
      \\
      p_{t,a,\text{PV}}^{\text{GF\\&LFC}\,\text{UP}}
       & : \text{GF\&LFC up-reserve power supplied by PV in area $a$ at time $t$ [MW]}
      \\
      p_{t,a,\text{PV}}^{\text{GF\\&LFC}\,\text{DOWN}}
       & : \text{GF\&LFC down-reserve power supplied by PV in area $a$ at time $t$ [MW]}
      \\
      p_{t,a,\text{PV}}^{\text{GF\\&LFC}\,\text{UP, req}}
       & : \text{Required GF\&LFC up-reserve power attributable to PV in area $a$ at time $t$ [MW]}
      \\
      p_{t,a,\text{PV}}^{\text{GF\\&LFC}\,\text{DOWN, req}}
       & : \text{Required GF\&LFC down-reserve power attributable to PV in area $a$ at time $t$ [MW]}
      \\
      p_{t,a,\text{PV}}^{\text{Tert}\,\text{UP}}
       & : \text{Tertiary up-reserve power secured by PV in area $a$ at time $t$ [MW]}
      \\
      p_{t,a,\text{PV}}^{\text{Tert}\,\text{DOWN}}
       & : \text{Tertiary down-reserve power secured by PV in area $a$ at time $t$ [MW]}
      \\
      p_{t,a,\text{PV}}^{\text{Tert}\,\text{UP, req}}
       & : \text{Required tertiary up-reserve power attributable to PV in area $a$ at time $t$ [MW]}
      \\
      p_{t,a,\text{PV}}^{\text{Tert}\,\text{DOWN, req}}
       & : \text{Required tertiary down-reserve power attributable to PV in area $a$ at time $t$ [MW]}
\end{array}
$$
