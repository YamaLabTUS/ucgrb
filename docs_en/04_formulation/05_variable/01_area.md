# Decision variables for area

Unless otherwise noted, all variables x are non-negative real numbers. $x \in R_{+}$ (non-negative real numbers)

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
      p_{t,a}^{\text{short}}
       & : \text{Average supply shortages occurring in area $a$ at time $t$ [MW]}
      \\
      p_{t,a}^{\text{surplus}}
       & : \text{Average supply surplus generated in area  $a$ at time $t$ [MW]}
      \\
      p_{t,a}^{\text{Tert}\,\text{UP, short}}
       & : \text{Shortages of tertiary up reserve that occurs in area  $a$ at time $t$ [MW]}
      \\
      p_{t,a}^{\text{Tert}\,\text{DOWN, short}}
       & : \text{Shortages of tertiary down reserve that occurs in area  $a$ at time $t$ [MW]}
\end{array}
$$
